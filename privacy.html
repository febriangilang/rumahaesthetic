Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Rumah Aesthetic Scheduler application ("the App") handles information. The App is a personal tool operated by Rumah Aesthetic to schedule and publish our own product pins to our own Pinterest business account through the Pinterest API.

Who operates the App

Operator: Rumah Aesthetic

Contact: mail.akalimitasi@gmail.com

What the App does

The App generates image pins for home-decor products and publishes them, on a schedule, to boards owned by the operator's own Pinterest account. It is used only by the operator, for the operator's own account.

Information we access

  • Pinterest authorization: an OAuth access token and refresh token issued by Pinterest when the operator authorizes the App, used to create pins on the operator's own boards.
  • Board and pin identifiers: the IDs of the operator's own boards and the pins the App creates.
  • The App does not access, read, or store the pins, boards, or personal data of any other Pinterest user.

How information is used

  • Solely to create and publish pins on the operator's own Pinterest account.
  • The App does not sell, share, rent, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • The App does not use data for advertising, profiling, or analytics about other people.

Where information is stored

Authorization tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are used only to communicate with the Pinterest API. They are not uploaded to any external server other than Pinterest itself.

Third-party services

  • Pinterest API — used to publish pins. Pinterest's own privacy policy governs data held by Pinterest.
  • Pins may contain affiliate links to Shopee product pages. Visiting those links is subject to Shopee's own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is in use and can be deleted at any time by removing the local token file. The operator can also revoke the App's access from Pinterest account settings, which immediately invalidates the tokens.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age required by Pinterest's terms.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
